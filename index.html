Common questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How long does setup take? +
Most businesses are live within one hour. We add a single line of code to your existing website — no redesign, no downtime, no technical expertise required.
Does it work with any website? +
Yes. Mintt works with any website — Wix, WordPress, Squarespace, custom HTML, Shopify, or anything else. If it has a website, we can add Mintt.
What happens when a lead comes in? +
You receive an instant email with the customer's name, contact details, and the full conversation — scored HOT or COLD. Your dashboard updates in real time. You just follow up and close.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. We don't believe in locking businesses in — we believe in earning your business every month.
Is my customer data private? +
Absolutely. All data is stored on Canadian servers and never sold or shared with third parties. We are compliant with PIPEDA (Canada's federal privacy law).
Do you offer a free trial? +
The Starter plan includes a 14-day free trial. We also offer a free live demo where we show you exactly how Mintt works with your business — book one below, it takes 20 minutes and there's no commitment.
